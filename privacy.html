Privacy & data

Progress photos are personal. Here is how FitLens protects them.

Who can see your media

Only you and your coach. Files are stored in a private Azure Blob Storage container with no public access. When you open a photo or video, FitLens creates a read-only link that expires after 15 minutes.

Where your data is stored

Media and its details are stored in Microsoft Azure data centres in Europe (France Central), so data stays under UK and EU GDPR protection. Files are encrypted at rest.

What we store

  • The photo or video you upload
  • Its caption, training week, upload date and file size
  • Your coach’s comments

Deleting your data

Use Delete on any check-in. FitLens removes both the file from storage and its record from the database. This cannot be undone.

Consent

Body images can count as health data. Only upload images you are comfortable sharing with your coach, and you can withdraw them at any time by deleting them.

Prototype notice

This is a university prototype (COM682). Sign-in is simulated by choosing a role; a production version would use Microsoft Entra ID authentication.
